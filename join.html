智慧神經人因工程實驗室（CNElab）隸屬國立清華大學工業工程與工程管理學系，致力於了解大腦如何在真實情境下支撐注意力、決策與社會互動。我們結合腦波（EEG）、腦機介面（BCI）與人工智慧技術，研究虛擬實境空間導航、多工負荷、教育情境與臨床評估中的人類表現，並發展閉迴路腦機介面與多模態深度學習模型。我們的長期目標是打造以人為本的智慧神經科技，協助提升日常生活與工業4.0場域中的安全、學習與身心健康。

我們正在招募對神經人因工程（Neuroergonomics）充滿熱情的研究生與大學生！研究主題涵蓋腦機介面（Brain–Computer Interface, BCI）與人工智慧（AI），結合神經科學與工程技術，探索人腦在互動、決策與學習中的運作機制。在這裡，你將接觸實際的腦波與多模態資料，設計虛擬實境與真實情境中的實驗，並運用深度學習模型分析注意力、動作協調與社會互動。

The Laboratory for Computational NeuroErgonomics (CNElab) at National Tsing Hua University investigates how the brain supports attention, decision-making, and social interaction in realistic tasks. We combine EEG, brain-computer interfaces, and artificial intelligence to understand and enhance human performance in settings such as virtual reality navigation, multitasking, education, and clinical assessment. Our goal is to create human-centered neurotechnology that improves safety, learning, and well-being in everyday life and Industry 4.0 environments.

Calling curious minds! We're looking for grad and undergrad students excited about neuroergonomics, BCI, and AI. At CNElab, you will work with real EEG and multimodal data, design experiments in virtual reality and real-world environments, and develop AI models to study attention, motor control, and social interaction in healthy and clinical populations.

Come explore how the brain connects with technology and shapes human behavior. We'll be in touch soon.
