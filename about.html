About Us

Laboratory for Computational NeuroErgonomics · 智慧神經人因工程實驗室

Mission

Enhance human performance via AI-BCI

The Laboratory for Computational NeuroErgonomics (CNElab) is part of the Department of Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management, College of Engineering, at National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan. We investigate how the brain supports attention, decision-making, and social interaction in realistic tasks, combining EEG, brain-computer interfaces (BCI), and artificial intelligence.

我們正在招募對神經人因工程（Neuroergonomics）充滿熱情的研究生與大學生！研究主題涵蓋腦機介面（Brain–Computer Interface, BCI）與人工智慧（AI），結合神經科學與工程技術，探索人腦在互動、決策與學習中的運作機制。

What you can do in CNElab

Design EEG and BCI experiments.

Build deep learning and x-to-x multimodal models that link brain activity with behavior and other biosignals.

Collaborate with hospitals, schools, and industry partners on projects in education, mental health, and human-machine interaction.

Present your work at conferences and contribute to journal papers as part of an interdisciplinary team.

Our long-term goal is to build human-centered neurotechnology that improves safety, learning, and well-being — both in everyday life and in Industry 4.0 settings.
