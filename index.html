About CNElab

Enhance human performance via AI-BCI

The Laboratory for Computational NeuroErgonomics (CNElab), based in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management at National Tsing Hua University, investigates how the brain supports attention, decision-making, and social interaction in realistic tasks — combining EEG, brain-computer interfaces, and AI.
