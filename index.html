About CNElab

Decode the Brain, Enhance Performance

Based in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management at National Tsing Hua University, the Laboratory for Computational NeuroErgonomics (CNElab) investigates how the human brain drives attention, decision-making, and social interaction in real-world environments. By converging brain-computer interfaces (BCI) with advanced artificial intelligence, we are pioneering the next generation of human-computer interaction.

Brain-Computer Interface · Biomedical Signal Processing · Artificial Intelligence · Data Mining · Machine Learning · Neuroergonomics

What you can do in CNElab

Design EEG and BCI experiments.

Build deep learning and x-to-x multimodal models that link brain activity with behavior and other biosignals.

Collaborate with hospitals, schools, and industry partners on projects in education, mental health, and human-machine interaction.

Present your work at conferences and contribute to journal papers as part of an interdisciplinary team.
